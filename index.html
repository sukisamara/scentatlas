An online scent diary

Scent Atlas is my online diary of how places smell. I'm curious about people, culture, history and heritage, and about the everyday ways a culture expresses itself and passes on its wisdom: in kitchens, gardens, temples and markets. Smells are easy to lose when a place changes, so this is my small way of keeping the world's scent stories on record, one city at a time, starting with Singapore.

My background is in consumer insight, trends and foresight.

With a curious nose, Suki
