An online scent diary

I grew up in Singapore, where a single walk can take you from jasmine garlands in Little India to attar shops in Kampong Gelam and joss smoke drifting out of a Chinatown temple. Each of those smells carries a story: who arrived here, what they traded, what they prayed with and what they cooked for the people they loved. I'm curious about people, culture, history and heritage, and about the quiet ways a culture passes on its wisdom, in kitchens, gardens, temples and markets.

Smells are easy to lose when a place changes, so Scent Atlas is my online diary for keeping the world's scent stories, one city at a time, starting with home. It grows out of years of noticing what people want and where taste is heading, through work in consumer insight, trends and foresight.

With a curious nose & lots of love, Suki
